    How to play
    1. Every swipe does two things: the track grows one cell in that direction and swallows the tile it lands on, then all loose tiles slide and merge like 2048.
    2. Two equal pieces merge into a bigger one: Bump, Hill, Drop, Helix, Loop, Corkscrew, Mega Loop.
    3. Chain reactions: a fresh merge grabs a matching neighbor and merges again, and again. Every chain link gives the whole queue +1 patience.
    4. Laid track is a wall. Tiles trapped behind it are gone for good.
    5. Swipe back into the station to open the ride. Riders lose patience every swipe, so don't dawdle.

    Arrow keys or WASD to swipe, Z to undo. Seed

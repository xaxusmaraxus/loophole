      Loophole

      How to play

      1. Swipe to slide and merge tiles like 2048: Airtime Hill, Lift Hill, Helix, Vertical Loop, Corkscrew, Cobra Roll, Top Hat.
      2. Lift Hills score nothing on their own: they store height. The next element after a chain of lifts hits harder (×2 after one lift, ×3 after two…). Height left over at the end of the ride becomes a finale drop into the station.
      3. Chain reactions: a fresh merge grabs a matching neighbor and merges again, and again.
      4. Hype sells tickets. Each day starts with a tiny loop over the station and a few tickets in your wallet. The train waits at the station while you build; every move, the ride's hype draws guests to the gate and each one buys a ticket (shown under your wallet: guests per move × ticket price). A wilder ride draws more guests and charges more.
      5. Tap a glowing tile to buy it into the ride. Its price tag shows what it costs: gold when you can afford it. The loop bulges out over that tile and the cell beside it. Bigger pieces cost more and earn more, and every piece makes the next one 12% pricier. Merge first (merging is free) to make better pieces.
      6. Pukes are jackpots. When you close the park, everyone in line rides once and pays the full rating for every puke. A bigger, wilder ride draws a bigger crowd.
      7. Close the park with at least the target in hand. Everyone in line rides once; the day's score is your wallet at closing plus what their pukes pay. Every swipe drops in a new tile; if nothing can slide and the loop can't grow, it's gridlock and the park closes by itself.
      8. A season is three parks, then the Grand Opening. Pick your route on each park's map: days, VIP days, storms, shops, repairs and treasure, then the park's boss.
      9. Make them puke. Every puke pays excitement × multiplier. Each rider has a stomach size and a weakness, so build for the crowd in line (hover a guest to meet them). A shuttle hits every piece twice but pays half.
      10. Your park plot: attractions and upgrades sit on a grid you arrange between days. Attractions score top row first, left to right, and each gets +1 multiplier for every touching spot of its theme. Targets climb fast, so build combos.
      11. The capsule machine (in shops and treasure stops) sells eggs. Crack one and pick: attractions, tools, or a bus tour that adds a rider type to your line every morning.
      12. Park pieces: chain reactions of two links or more make the park's own pieces: 🌀 spinning cars in the Meadow (nausea ×1.5), 💧 water flumes on the Boardwalk (+1 multiplier), 🦇 hanging track in the Hollow (+3 thrill, counts as upside down). Big combos (6, 9, 12 merges in one swipe) pay a free special piece.
      13. Each park ends with a boss who bends the rules for the day. Make them puke enough times to break their composure: they ride every lap until they break. Breaking a boss grows your plot and drops a legendary attraction.
      14. Between days, pick an upgrade (permanent) or a tool (Paint Can, Crane, Dynamite, Coffee, Megaphone) to spend when it counts.

      Keys: arrows or WASD swipe, click or tap a glowing tile to buy it into the ride, 1–5 use tools, Esc cancels a tool, Enter closes the park, Z undoes. Seed

    In line 0

    Each rider pukes every time the ride's nausea passes their stomach (up to 5 times). The ride you have standing draws a crowd with every swipe, and chain reactions draw one rider per link.

      How to play
      1. Swipe to slide and merge tiles like 2048: Bump, Hill, Drop, Helix, Loop, Corkscrew, Mega Loop.
      2. Chain reactions: a fresh merge grabs a matching neighbor and merges again, and again.
      3. Tap a highlighted cell to build: the tile there becomes that piece of track. Track grows from both sides of the station (the red and blue pennants).
      4. When the pennants meet, open the full circuit. Or cash out any time as a shuttle for half.
      5. Every swipe uses daylight; building is free. After sunset you can't swipe, but you can keep building and open the ride whenever you're ready.
      6. A season is three parks, then the Grand Opening. Pick your route on each park's map: days, VIP days, storms, shops, repairs and treasure, then the park's boss.
      7. Make them puke. Every puke pays excitement × multiplier. Each rider has a stomach size and a weakness, so build for the crowd in line. A shuttle hits every piece twice but pays half.
      8. Attractions (up to 5) add excitement or multiplier, or multiply it, left to right. Targets climb fast, so build combos.
      9. Each park ends with a boss: a huge or seasoned rider with a massive stomach. Make them puke to clear the park.
      10. Between days, pick an upgrade (permanent) or a tool (Paint Can, Crane, Dynamite, Coffee, Megaphone) to spend when it counts.

      Keys: arrows or WASD swipe. Shift + arrows build from the waving pennant, Tab switches pennant, 1–5 use tools, Esc cancels a tool, Enter opens the ride, Z undoes. Seed
